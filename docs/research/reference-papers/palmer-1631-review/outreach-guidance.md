# Palmer review findings and outreach guidance

## Evidence for the invitation

In the five-month case, the dry candidate reaches about −0.6323 at zero-based index 8. The Wells lineage establishes a drought and the standard Palmer lineage keeps it incipient. The first material difference in final PDSI appears at index 9 after backtracking. Palmer and Alley explicitly include equality at ±1.00. Wells describes reaching ±0.5 and leaves the detailed backtracking procedure open. The reviewer can judge how far those passages support the statement about the exact reported values. [One-page evidence sheet](evidence-sheet.md)

The broader audit found that all 3,084 matched decisions followed the expected threshold magnitudes. Only Wells established a spell in 1,326 division-months across 336 divisions, including 772 dry and 554 wet cases. Final PDSI differed in the decision month in one of those 1,326 cases. Changing only the standard threshold produced a 91.0% net reduction in material PDSI disagreements over the full fixture and introduced 631 differences in previously agreeing months. Use these counts to describe implementation coverage under fixed factors. The observations come from one historical fixture and may be correlated. [Coverage assessment](coverage-assessment.md)
